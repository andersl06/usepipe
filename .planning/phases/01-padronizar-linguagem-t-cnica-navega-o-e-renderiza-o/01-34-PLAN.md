Fixtures from the pre-rename DB + goldens recorded on the pre-rename tree are the ground truth.
Output: dump script, redacted fixtures + manifest, `jsonb-keys.ts` with tests and baseline, `jsonb-compat.test.ts` with goldens.

Model routing: Claude (Sonnet). No Codex (fixtures may contain dev data; they never leave the machine).
</objective>
