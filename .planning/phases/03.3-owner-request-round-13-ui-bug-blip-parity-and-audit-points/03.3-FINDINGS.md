### Proven (Blip evidence)
- Selects on application/product-updates and analytics/data-extractor were fixed earlier on this branch (commits e9c669ee, cb8bc64a): `global.css` scopes `.selection-control` inside `.cf-campo`/`.ct-campo` wrappers.

| Class | Files | Resolution |
|-------|-------|------------|
| ct-rotulo, ct-nome, ct-section, ct-cards | contract.css, contacts.css | contract renamed to `ctr-*` (contract/page.tsx); contacts keeps `ct-*` |
| ct-info, ct-aviso | contract.css, contacts.css (+ conteudos.css for ct-info) | contract renamed to `ctr-*` (contract/page.tsx, members/page.tsx) |
| ct-titulo, ct-card, ct-card-text | contract.css, conteudos.css | contract renamed to `ctr-*` and contents renamed to `cnt-*` |
| ct-info, ct-header-section, ct-header-actions, ct-botao-icone, ct-container, ct-divisor, ct-voltar | contacts.css, conteudos.css | contents renamed to `cnt-*` (conteudos.css, contents/tela.tsx, resources/recursos.tsx); contacts keeps `ct-*` |
| ct-campo / cf-campo in global.css | global.css vs conteudos.css / settings.css | not a collision: global.css only uses them as ancestor scope (`.ct-campo .selection-control`) |

Legacy collisions outside cf-/ct- (not touched, frozen in `LEGACY_COLLISIONS`, may only shrink): tbl-legenda, tbl-search, cl-sel, pt-obra, pt-links-obra, ig-interruptor, cr-botao, an-bp-btn, an-card-titulo, fx-column, ph-cabecalho, ph-conteudo, ph-titulo, ph-direita.
